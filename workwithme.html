I help small teams improve recurring reporting, unreliable data workflows, and focused internal processes.

Projects are scoped around a specific problem, practical deliverables, and a solution that is documented and maintainable after handoff.

Services

Reporting Automation

For teams manually combining spreadsheets, database exports, or API data every week or month.

I can help replace repetitive reporting work with a dependable process that collects, cleans, summarizes, and delivers the information you need.

Typical projects: Scheduled reports, recurring data imports, automated summaries, and spreadsheet or database consolidation.

Workflow Improvement

For teams with fragile scripts, inconsistent spreadsheets, duplicated data, or unreliable reporting.

I can help turn an error-prone process into a clearer, more maintainable workflow with better structure, validation, and documentation.

Typical projects: Workflow cleanup, data validation, script improvement, and spreadsheet process redesign.

Internal Tools

For teams that need a lightweight dashboard, data-entry tool, review interface, or operational utility within a defined scope.

I can build a focused tool that makes an existing workflow easier to manage without requiring a full software product.

Typical projects: Review dashboards, data-entry interfaces, operational utilities, and lightweight internal applications.

Is This a Good Fit?

I may be a good fit when you have a defined workflow or recurring problem that needs focused technical help.

  • A report is assembled manually every week or month.
  • Data needs to be combined from multiple sources automatically.
  • An existing script works inconsistently or is difficult to maintain.
  • A spreadsheet has become complicated or error-prone.
  • Your team needs a lightweight dashboard or operational tool.
  • You need a focused project with clearly defined deliverables.

Tell Me About Your Project

A few sentences are enough to get started. Helpful details include what you do now, how often you do it, where the data comes from, and what you would like the finished workflow or tool to produce. Support can be tailored to the work you actually need, whether that is a one-time project or ongoing improvements.

I'll review your note personally and reply with any initial questions. If the project looks like a good fit, we'll clarify the requirements, agree on a defined scope, and work directly together through delivery.
