These case studies show how I approach recurring reporting, data workflow, and custom data problems - from understanding the bottleneck through implementation, delivery, and handoff.

Reporting Automation

Automating Executive Reporting Across Multiple Data Sources

A recurring executive reporting process required dozens of hours of manual work each month across APIs, CSV files, website screenshots, Excel, and PowerPoint. I designed and built a Python-based workflow automated the process from data collection to delivery, reducing report generation from days to about 20 minutes.

Data Workflow Improvement

Building a Reliable Data Migration and Validation Workflow

A large enterprise client needed to move decades of equity-plan history from a legacy platform into a new production system without compromising financial or transactional integrity. I managed a validation-first migration process using T-SQL identified and remedied historical inconsistencies before migration then reconciled balances, transactions, and financial totals after transformation.

Custom Data Solutions

Translating Executive Reporting Goals into a Practical Data Solution

Executive stakeholders had proposed dozens of new reporting fields, but the organization first needed to understand what its existing data could reliably support. I evaluated each request for feasibility and effort, documented available and missing data, and provided SQL logic and requirements that helped leadership prioritize immediate reporting needs and longer-term development.
